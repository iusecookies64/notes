Here are the Unicode hex codes for the most common Greek letters and mathematical symbols used in STEM fields.

## 1. The Greek Alphabet (Common Math Constants & Variables)

Remember the trick: All standard Greek letters start with `03`. Capital versions generally use `9`, while lowercase versions use `B` or `C`.

|Symbol Name|Capital|Code|Lowercase|Code|Quick Mnemonic for Lowercase|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Alpha|Α|`0391`|α|`03B1`|1st letter ends in 1|
|Beta|Β|`0392`|β|`03B2`|2nd letter ends in 2|
|Gamma|Γ|`0393`|γ|`03B3`|3rd letter ends in 3|
|Delta|Δ|`0394`|δ|`03B4`|Comes B4 (Before)|
|Epsilon|Ε|`0395`|ε|`03B5`|5th letter ends in 5|
|Theta|Θ|`0390`|θ|`03B8`|Shaped like an 8, ends in 8|
|Lambda|Λ|`039B`|λ|`03BB`|Double Bs|
|Mu (Micro)|Μ|`039C`|μ|`03BC`|Follows Lambda (`B` -> `C`)|
|Pi|Π|`03A0`|π|`03C0`|Crosses over into the C0s|
|Sigma (Sum)|Σ|`03A3`|σ|`03C3`|Both end in 3|
|Omega|Ω|`03A9`|ω|`03C9`|The final letter, both end in 9|

## 2. Operators, Calculus, & Logic Symbols

These symbols do not have capital or lowercase versions, but they are essential for typing equations. They live in different Unicode blocks, but you can group them by their starting digits to remember them easily.

## The `22` Block (Calculus & Relations)

Most core operational symbols start with `22`.

- `221E` ➔ ∞ (Infinity) — _Mnemonic: "22" followed by "1" and an "E" shape_
- `2202` ➔ ∂ (Partial Derivative)
- `222B` ➔ ∫ (Integral)
- `2248` ➔ ≈ (Approximately equal)
- `2260` ➔ ≠ (Not equal to)
- `2264` ➔ ≤ (Less than or equal to)
- `2265` ➔ ≥ (Greater than or equal to)

## The Set Theory & Logic Group

- `2208` ➔ ∈ (Element of / Belongs to)
- `2209` ➔ ∉ (Not an element of)
- `2286` ➔ ⊆ (Subset of)
- `211D` ➔ ℝ (Real numbers set — starts with `21`)

## How to Practice

Try practicing directly in your terminal or a text editor using the `Ctrl + Shift + U` sequence:

1. `Ctrl+Shift+U` ➔ `03C0` ➔ `Enter` (Should give you π)
2. `Ctrl+Shift+U` ➔ `221E` ➔ `Enter` (Should give you ∞)
