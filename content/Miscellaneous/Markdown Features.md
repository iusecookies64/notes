### Common Callout Types and Their Intended Use:

- **Note:** General information, a reminder, or a detail that's good to know.
    
- **Tip / Hint:** A helpful suggestion, a shortcut, or a piece of advice.
    
- **Info:** Standard information that's relevant to the topic.
    
- **Success / Done:** Indicates a successful action, a completed task, or positive outcome.
    
- **Question / Help:** Poses a question, or points to a need for assistance or clarification.
    
- **Warning / Caution / Attention:** Alerts the reader to a potential risk, a common pitfall, or a detail that requires special care.
    
- **Failure / Error / Bug:** Highlights a problem, an error, or a known bug.
    
- **Danger:** A strong warning for something that could cause significant harm or loss.
    
- **Quote / Cite:** Used to format a direct quote from a source.
    
- **Example:** Presents an example of code, syntax, or a concept in action.
    
- **Abstract / Summary / TLDR:** Provides a brief summary of a longer text.

#### Example Markdown Syntax

The general syntax for a callout is a blockquote with a special tag: `> [!TYPE]`.

Example:

> [!NOTE] This is a note.

You can also add a custom title by placing it after the type tag.

Example:

> [!TIP] Pro Tip
>This is a handy tip for your workflow.
