### 16 September 2025

1. **Bigotry:** obstinate or unreasonable attachment to a belief, opinion, or faction in particular prejudice against a person or group of people.
2. **Prejudice:** preconceived opinion that is not based on reason or actual experience.
3. **Obstinate:** stubbornly refusing to change one's opinion or course of action, despite attempts to persuade one to do so.
4. **Despite:**** without being affected by.
5. **Faction:** a small organized **dissenting** group within a larger one, especially in politics.
6. **Dissenting:** holding or expressing views that are at variance with those that are commonly or officially held.
7. **What is MIME type?** It stands for **Multipurpose Internet Mail Extension** which was introduces as a way to add different data type support for emails as before it only text emails containing ASCII characters could be sent. This adds headers in the emails that helps the system know the nature and format of the file or data it is receiving. Although introduced as a way to extend the types of data that could be send via email, it became a standard for other internet types of communications on the internet.
